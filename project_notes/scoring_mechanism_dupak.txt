# DUPAK Scoring Mechanism Documentation
# Based on analysis of mockdata_dupak.xlsx and codebase

## ================================================================================
## 1. BASIC FORMULA: ANGKA KREDIT (KUM) CALCULATION
## ================================================================================

For Each Submission:
    Angka Kredit Total = Nilai Baku × Volume

Where:
- Nilai Baku    = Base credit value from reference table (ref_nilai_kum_baku)
- Volume       = Number of units/activities performed

### Examples from mockdata:

| Activity                              | Nilai Baku | Volume | Calculation | Result |
|---------------------------------------|------------|--------|-------------|--------|
| Pendidikan S1                        | 100        | 1      | 1 × 100     | 100    |
| Pendidikan S2                         | 50         | 1      | 1 × 50      | 50     |
| MK 4 SKS, 2 classes                   | 1          | 4×2=8  | 8 × 1       | 8      |
| Thesis guidance (Main Supervisor)     | 1          | 1      | 1 × 1       | 1      |
| Thesis guidance (Assistant Supervisor)| 0.5        | 1      | 1 × 0.5     | 0.5    |
| Exam committee (Chairman)             | 1          | 1      | 1 × 1       | 1      |
| Exam committee (Member)              | 0.5        | 1      | 1 × 0.5     | 0.5    |

## ================================================================================
## 2. SCORING MECHANISM FOR EACH SUBMISSION (TPAK VALIDATION)
## ================================================================================

When TPAK evaluates each activity detail, the formula is:

    Nilai Angka Kredit (Approved) = Angka Kredit Total × (Score % / 100)

Parameters:
- score (0-100) : Percentage given by TPAK evaluator
- flag         : OK | Doubt | Fake

### Logic Table:

| Flag  | Status    | Meaning             | Credit Given           |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| OK    | approved  | Valid activity     | Full × Score%         |
| Doubt | revision  | Need revision     | 0 (pending)           |
| Fake  | rejected  | Invalid/fake       | 0                     |

## ================================================================================
## 3. VOLUME-BASED SCORING (SPECIAL CASES)
## ================================================================================

### a. Teaching (Melaksanakan Perkuliahan):
- SKS ≤ 10  : Full score (1 per SKS-class)
- SKS > 10  : Split calculation with 0.5 factor for overload

**THE DOUBLE CALCULATION MECHANISM (SKS > 10):**

When semester total SKS > 10, Excel stores TWO calculations in ONE row:
- Each cell contains two values separated by newlines "\n"
- First line = normal portion (factor 1.0)
- Second line = overload portion (factor 0.5)

Actual data from mockdata (Row 102):
    Cell[4] = "4 x 1\n4 x 1"     → Two volume entries
    Cell[5] = "1\n0.5"            → Two credit values  
    Cell[6] = "4x1x1=4\n4x1x0.5=2" → Two calculations
    
    Excel display columns:
    - [8]=4, [9]=2, [10]=6  → "4" from normal, "2" from overload, "6" = Total

Formula for DOUBLE calculation:
    Total_SKS = SKS × kelas
    
    IF Total_SKS > 10:
        Normal_Portion  = MIN(Total_SKS, 10) × 1.0 = up to 10
        Overload_Portion = MAX(0, Total_SKS - 10) × 0.5 = remaining × 0.5
        Total_KUM       = Normal + Overload
    
    ELSE:
        Total_KUM = Total_SKS × 1.0

Example 1 - Row 102 (Semester 2021/2022 with 11 SKS total):
    Subject: 4 SKS, 1 class = 4 total
    - Part 1: 4 × 1 × 1.0 = 4 (normal)
    - Part 2: 4 × 1 × 0.5 = 2 (overload)
    - Total: 6

Example 2 - Row 109 (Semester 2022/2023 with 11 SKS total):
    Subject: 4 SKS, 2 classes = 8 total
    - Part 1: 6 × 1 × 1.0 = 6 (first 6 SKS of 8)
    - Part 2: 2 × 1 × 0.5 = 1 (remaining 2 SKS × 0.5)
    - Total: 7

**IMPORTANT - Why TWO entries per row:**

The "Jumlah" column (column G/10) shows combined total, but TPAK validation 
needs to evaluate BOTH the normal and overload portions separately. Each portion 
can have different validation status (OK/Doubt/Fake) and scores.

For proper implementation, when semester SKS > 10, store TWO detail records:
1. Record A: angka_kredit with factor 1.0 for normal portion
2. Record B: angka_kredit with factor 0.5 for overload portion

### b. Thesis/Dissertation Guidance (Membimbing Skripsi/Tesis):
- Main Supervisor (Pembimbing Utama)     : Full 1.0
- Assistant Supervisor (Pembimbing Pendamping): 0.5

### c. Exam Committee (Bertugas sebagai Penguji Ujian):
- Chairman (Ketua Penguji)  : Full 1.0
- Member (Anggota Penguji)   : 0.5

## ================================================================================
## 5. TOTAL KUM CALCULATION
## ================================================================================

Total KUM = Sum of all approved activities across categories:

    Jumlah I (Pendidikan)         = Pendidikan Formal + Diklat Pra Jabatan
    Jumlah II (Pelaksanaan Pend.)  = A+B+C+D+E+F+G+H+I+J+K+L+M
    Jumlah Total                  = Jumlah I + Jumlah II

From mockdata example (Row 1224):
    Jumlah I   = 200 (S1+S2+S3 education)
    Jumlah II = 419 (all teaching activities)
    Jumlah    = 619

## ================================================================================
## 6. IMPLEMENTATION IN CODE
## ================================================================================

### a. Storing Activity (DetilPengajuanController):

    $nilaiBaku = $refInput->nilai_baku;  // From ref_jenis_input
    $volume = $request->input('volume', 1);
    $detail->angka_kredit_murni = $nilaiBaku;
    $detail->angka_kredit_total = $nilaiBaku * $volume;

### b. TPAK Validation (ValidasiController):

    $scorePercent = $request->score / 100;
    $approvedCredit = $detail->angka_kredit_total * $scorePercent;

    // Save evaluation
    HasilEvaluasi::updateOrCreate([
        'nilai_angka_kredit' => $approvedCredit,
        'status_evaluasi' => $flag, // OK/Doubt/Fake
    ]);

## ================================================================================
## 7. SUMMARY FLOW
## ================================================================================

1. Dosen submits activity
   → Stores with: angka_kredit_total = nilai_baku × volume

2. TPAK evaluates
   → Gives score (0-100%) and flag (OK/Doubt/Fake)

3. Calculate approved credit
   → approved = total × (score/100)

4. Sum all approved
   → Total KUM for DUPAK application
